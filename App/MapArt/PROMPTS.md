# Terrain atlas

Use case: historical-scene / game terrain sprite atlas. Create ORIGINAL production-ready terrain assets for a late-Han Chinese grand strategy map with the visual mood of a realistic historical strategy game's oblique aerial terrain. Not a screenshot, not UI. ONE transparent sprite atlas, EXACTLY 4 columns by 2 rows, equal square cells, landscape aspect ratio 2:1, ideally 2048x1024. Eight separate fully isolated objects on truly transparent background. Each object centered in its own cell with generous transparent gutter; nothing may cross a cell boundary. Objects all viewed from the same 45 degree elevated oblique camera, light from upper left, natural muted jade/olive/earth palette, painterly realistic high-detail 3D prerender look, no polygonal triangles, no cartoon cones, no pixel art. Top row left to right: (1) a rugged grey limestone mountain massif covered with green vegetation, three irregular connected peaks; (2) a wide folded forest-covered rocky mountain ridge, lower and rounder; (3) dramatic craggy sandstone peaks with forested foothills; (4) a high rocky mountain saddle with several uneven peaks, subtle mist. Bottom row left to right: (5) dense broadleaf woodland grove, many organic overlapping crowns; (6) a different mixed woodland grove with varying tree heights; (7) fortified ancient Chinese city, stone perimeter walls, gate towers, dark tiled roofs, compact dense buildings inside, no people, no flags; (8) small ancient Chinese market town, a few tiled-roof buildings and pavilion, no walls. IMPORTANT: each sprite has a small organic footprint, all terrain fades softly into transparency at ground edge, a subtle semitransparent shadow below/right, bottom of each object's ground footprint around 84% down its cell, highest peak/roof at least 10% from top. No visible grid, no frames, no labels, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no background landscape, no checkerboard painted into the image. The atlas must be directly usable as eight cropped transparent sprites in a game.

# Ground materials

Use case: game material texture atlas. Generate ONE original realistic painted terrain MATERIAL texture atlas for a historical Chinese grand strategy map. Square image, exactly two columns and two rows, four equal square quadrants with no gutters, no border, no labels. Top left: muted olive/jade grassland ground seen directly overhead, fine grass and very subtle soil mottling, relatively smooth, no trees or objects. Top right: dry olive grass mixed with ochre earth and tiny stone grain, same brightness and hue family as the top left, subtle patchiness, no objects. Bottom left: calm river water, muted turquoise/teal, fine ripples and subtle depth changes, no shore or land. Bottom right: deep sea water, muted blue teal, subdued fine wave streaks, no shore, no land. Entire atlas flat overhead texture, NO perspective, NO lighting gradients across quadrants, NO mountains, NO horizon, NO buildings, NO text, NO frames, NO UI. Natural realistic terrain texture at small game scale, restrained contrast and fine detail, not geometric, not pixel art, no decorative pattern. Each quadrant should appear as an edge-to-edge tileable continuous material. Consistent soft diffuse daylight, no cast shadows. Ideally 2048x2048.
